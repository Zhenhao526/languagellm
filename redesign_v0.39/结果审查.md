## 主要发现

- **three_resource_joint_formation**：Three resource-specific senders and a 343-code receiver can form grounded slot-wise protocols from random communication heads, while single-resource accuracy and joint success remain separable.
- **topology_effect**：Fixed, rotating and random partner schedules produce different tradeoffs between local grounded success and cross-partner token agreement.
- **complexity_boundary**：Moving from two to three resource slots exposes a composition bottleneck that is invisible in one-resource or two-resource aggregate success.

## 限制

- The visual frontends are inherited from earlier two-resource perceptual training and remain frozen.
- Each sender emits one token; this is a slot-composition probe rather than open-ended grammar.
- Shared reward and joint policy-gradient updates are centralized.
- The four-agent population, six sites and topology schedules are experimenter specified.
