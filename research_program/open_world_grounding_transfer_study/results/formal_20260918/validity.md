# Validity and limits

The formal matrix contains 54 parent runs, 27 single-new expansion children and 216 frozen-incumbent transfer runs across 9 seeds, three architectures, two fresh-receiver surface mappings and four role supports. Each shard was replay audited independently; all maximum absolute errors are 0 and incumbent hashes remain constant. The `(3,3)` double-new meaning is excluded from every transfer training stream.

The reverse surface mapping is a bijective tabular remapping of object attributes. It is not a rendered image, learned vision model or open-ended vocabulary. The fresh sender still receives semantic goal attributes, so the intervention isolates receiver grounding. The formal result should be reported as a cross-mechanism test of compositional cultural transmission under stable perceptual relabeling.
