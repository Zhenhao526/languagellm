# Validity and scope

- All three shard audits replayed the recorded streams and parameter updates with maximum absolute error `0.0`.
- The merged audit contains 54 parents, 81 children, 162,000 parent log rows, 243,000 child log rows, 216 parent checkpoints and 324 child checkpoints.
- Architecture-pair stream checks cover 243,000 rows; support-pair checks cover 162,000 rows. Visibility and population pairs are intentionally absent because this control freezes `hidden` and `aligned`.
- The control identifies a same-initialization effect and estimates the additional shared-update effect; with nine seeds, the latter is not decisive at the paired 95% interval.
- This archive is a tabular mechanism control, not evidence that the agents invented human language.
