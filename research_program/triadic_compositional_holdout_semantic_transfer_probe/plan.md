# Joint-combination holdout aligned/placebo transfer probe

This is a post-hoc probe over the audited `holdout_001` checkpoints. The new
receiver A was trained on 1,248 joint need combinations while 312 complete
semantic-orbit combinations were held out; B and C are frozen full-combination
parent policies. Within the heldout set, a case changes one sender's private
need along a supported kind or length neighbor while holding the public layout,
site owner, and the other needs fixed.

The aligned condition inserts the changed sender's first-window packet from the
neighboring heldout world into the target world. Placebo uses a cyclic donor
packet from the same sender, changed axis, and physical background. The legal
plan set of the aligned donor defines donor-only versus receiver-only transfer.
The primary exploratory comparison is A's aligned-minus-placebo plan transfer
against the mean of B and C on the same heldout cases. Silent conditions are
closed-channel controls and should be exactly zero.

This measures whether a newly adapted receiver's packet has directionally useful
content on unseen joint combinations. It is not a word-meaning, syntax,
intergenerational-transmission, or human-language-origin test.
