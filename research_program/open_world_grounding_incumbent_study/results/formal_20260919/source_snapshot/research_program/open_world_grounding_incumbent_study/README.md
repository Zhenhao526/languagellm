# Open-world grounding with incumbent receiver study

This follow-up crosses open-world compositional cultural transmission with a direct transfer of receiver grounding. Parents and expansion children follow the same 3x3-to-4x4 protocol as the preceding study, with `(3,3)` held out. Transfer replaces sender, receiver, both roles, or neither. A receiver replacement is either `fresh` (random receiver parameters) or `incumbent_receiver` (only the frozen incumbent receiver table is copied; sender parameters remain fresh).

Fresh receivers see either canonical `identity` object labels or the stable reverse permutation `[3,2,1,0]`. Semantic scenes, goals and random streams are paired. The primary interaction is initialization × surface mapping × role support × architecture, with `holistic` and fixed `factorized` policies. It asks whether an established receiver's grounding can be repaired under surface change while preserving a zero-shot double-new composition.

This is a tabular mechanism experiment, not a visual-language model. It is designed to separate parameter inheritance, perceptual re-grounding and compositional cultural transmission.
