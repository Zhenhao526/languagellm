# Ternary attribute and capacity extension

This formal matrix expands private attributes and resource objects from binary to ternary values. It compares capacity-matched `mono9` (one nine-valued token) with `tri3` (two three-valued slots), factorized and entangled targets, simultaneous and staged delivery, and paired live/silent channels across nine seeds.

The compact archive reports held-out natural return, live−silent effects, staged−simultaneous effects and the slot-recombination gap for `tri3`. It is a capacity-scaling control, not evidence of natural language. Raw per-update logs, checkpoints and the merged execution JSON were removed after audit.
